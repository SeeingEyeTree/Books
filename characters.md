# Characters

State of each character as of the end of Ch15.

## Main characters

### Angline Ruler (narrator)

Genius engineer, formerly at a military-funded lab with a blank check. Believes she is the best the world has ever seen and says so as a measurement, not a boast. Now the unreachable ruler of a growing industrial state in the Green, and publicly known on Earth.

- **Core traits:** Total confidence, little visible empathy, boundless enthusiasm ("an evil golden retriever"). Always prepared ("plays with a stacked deck"). Thinks in units, bottlenecks and second derivatives.
- **Beliefs:** The best should rule. Every limit in her life has been someone else's budget. Claims to be immortal; she isn't, it's pure self-regard. Honest when lying is unnecessary ("it's inefficient").
- **Wants:** Infinite motes (energy, money and tools nobody controls). Freedom to build without asking. A worthy opponent.
- **Loves:** Monologues and slides, spinning magnets, C4, trailers, new science, finding out. Boredom is her real enemy.
- **Hates:** Solar panels, committees, not knowing, numbers without units, her badge photo.
- **Signature items:** The coat with too many pockets. The push-rune glove. Heat discs. A pistol. C4, always.
- **Vanity:** Long skincare routine, "effortless beauty takes effort," the professional portrait.
- **Hidden softness:** Gives a flashlight to a child as "product testing." Asked Bethany how she felt. Let Nkemelu go rather than trap her. Never admits any of it ("Don't make it weird").
- **Family:** Hasn't called her own mother in nine years.
- **Arc so far:** Fugitive (Ch9) → builder hiding on Earth → unassailable and public (Ch15).

### Bethany

Scientist and former coworker under Alphonseo. Joined because the alternative was lava. Now runs the coast.

- **Background:** PhD in molecular biology. Worked in pharma, then for Alphonseo, testing compounds on cells.
- **Core traits:** Tired, deadpan, kind, doubts herself constantly and does the work anyway. Blinks when processing.
- **Role:** Runs the research loop (Ch7), found the rune grammar (Ch8) and the yeast result. Ran the coast while Angline negotiated (Ch13): built a container town, beat a cholera outbreak (33 of 34 survived). Owns the "people curve": schools, housing, hiring.
- **Titles:** Called "Doctor" by the coast (she isn't an MD). Angline calls her "Doctor Director."
- **Drive:** Seeing her own ideas built and their impact on people, with no committee in between. "She's also the reason I get to build something."
- **Toward Angline:** Once Operative B0, the disposable probe. Now senior staff, the one person who pushes back and wins (30 farms not 40; people curve). Still says "Great plan, Ms. Ruler."
- **Family:** Mother on Earth, who saw her on the news. Bethany called her once in secret (Ch13), which is how Nkemelu found the salt mine. Her mother fixed the kitchen clock (a message Nkemelu passed on). Angline's broadcast demands amnesty for her.
- **Sore spots:** Being burned (Ch3). The cannibalism argument (banned topic).
- **Status:** Lives on the coast; the last Earth door is sealed. "Like I live somewhere."

## Supporting cast: the Green

### Brask

Ancient scholar from Pinnacle's Lyceum, hired in Ch7. White hair standing straight up. Built a hand-cranked lodestone generator decades before Angline arrived.

- Taught Angline about harvest (Ch7). Cried when the first turbine spun.
- Kept the farm running alone when the first door closed (Ch9-10).
- **Runs the rune works** (from Ch15): CNC mills, megalith runes, drones. Leather apron, loupe on a chain, shouts in three dialects. His first lead role.
- Noticed Angline can see Shades on radar (Ch12). Recalculating.

### The Lady of House Verdane

Rules Verdane from the Lamp Hall atop her tower, beneath a 400-year-old great crystal. Thin, upright, never raises her voice.

- Now has Angline's electric lift (90 seconds to the top).
- Received Farm One in the Ch13 deal. "It was a clever price."
- Knows about Earth soldiers and watched the sub-basement four scar sealed (Ch15), attended by her own Shade.
- "I'll stop counting your doors, Ruler, and start counting you."
- On the ledger (debts) since the Ch13 threats.

### The Lord of Sable

About 35. Sharp cheekbones, sharper smile, better at arithmetic than anyone else in the Green. Inherited a House that was losing.

- Read Angline's dependency strategy back to her at the Stake Hall: "It isn't tribute. It's a leash."
- Suspects her steel isn't only for farms (visited the Red Ridge, Ch11).
- Hiring every rune carver at any price; thinks House vertical farms are three years away.
- His garrison's Shade watched the Sable Gate sealed (Ch15). He will do arithmetic.
- On the ledger (debts). Angline likes him less than she'd like to.

### The Lady of Ochre

Fifties, round, cheerful, sharp-eyed, honey-colored rings. Greets like a friend, watches like a merchant. Leased Angline the Red Ridge (99 years, 1 in 20). Mentioned that leases have been broken before. Building an 8-floor stone-and-bamboo tower. On the ledger (debts).

### Oren

House Verdane collector turned liaison. Narrow, ink-stained cuffs, paid by the hour. Bought the first flashlight for 60 motes. Loves riding the train; grips the rail in the lift.

### Marit

Elder of Shingle, about 60, short and broad, face like weathered teak, two fingers lost to the Glass. Blunt, sharp, knows every family and feud on the coast. Sits in on everything uninvited. Best in the first class at reading controller screens.

### The girl

About 8, gap where her front teeth should be. Follows Angline around Shingle. Has the flashlight ("product testing"). Unnamed so far.

### Shades (named characters)

- **The Lady's Shade:** A woman in plain gray who shimmers faintly at the edge of light. Watched the sealing.
- **Sable's garrison Shade:** A thin young man, rarely speaks. Watched the Sable Gate vanish.

## Supporting cast: Earth

### Detective Inspector Nkemelu

Forties, gray suit, short hair, paper files with an elastic band ("paper doesn't get hacked, and it burns"). The only person who ever checked Helsinki.

- Investigated Alphonseo's disappearance on her own time after Rourke's people closed the case.
- Found the salt mine from one phone call. Angline hired her with the truth and two cases (Ch14).
- Found and helped seal three natural thin places (Ch15).
- Refused to be part of threatening Brussels. Returned the mote and went home to write down everything, including that Angline told her the truth about Alphonseo.
- Angline has no ledger column for her. "I'm going to have to make one."
- Keeps her warrant card. Would arrest Angline if she could.

### Colonel Rourke

Military liaison who believed the portal belonged to him and never visited it. Three flags in his office. Wanted a door; tried to reopen sub-basement four (failed, Ch15). On the ledger: one day, a door in his office.

### Gerald

Facilities, eleven years. Clipboard, reads utility bills for pleasure, loses every argument to nouns he can't picture. Let Rourke into sub-basement four (Ch9). Became Nkemelu's inside source (Ch14). Ledger: "Gerald gets nothing." Gerald found something to do with his nothing.

### Others on Earth

- **Alphonseo:** Angline's boss, shot in Ch1, disposed of in the lava pool (Ch5). Cover story: a conference in Helsinki. His sister reported him missing.
- **Dr. Halvorsen:** Director of research, Alphonseo's boss, dying plant on her windowsill.
- **Keith:** Military auditor. Four hours over a per diem. Credit column: integrity.
- **Ottoline Maas:** About 70, coin dealer in a port city. Melts and recasts Pinnacle silver for 12%. Asks one question per deal. Her banks handled the final Kessel purchases.
- **Janis:** Sold the secondhand Latvian turbine, in pieces.
- **The loan officer:** Priya's husband. Credit column.
- **The portrait photographer:** Europe's best. Signed an agreement longer than most treaties.
- **Bethany's mother:** Kitchen clock (now fixed), certificate on the fridge.

## Billy the APC

Angline's Ace. Amphibious, broad, blunt-nosed, flat gray-green. 35mm autocannon (modified to 10,000 rounds/min), two missile launchers, a directed energy weapon, a 1 MW D-T fusion reactor built in a Geneva hotel room in three days. Radar (the one sense Shades can't fool). Tows trailers now. Keeps ~10 g of tritium.
