# Continuity Flags and Open Questions

Items marked `[x]` are resolved (decision recorded). Items marked `[ ]` are open.

## Needs fixing before/during Ch16

- [ ] **Mote output inconsistency.** Ch15 states the Brussels veil (~900,000 motes) cost "more than a week" of total output, implying total daily output around ~130,000/day. But by Ch11 twelve farms were under simultaneous construction, the farm-race plan (Ch14) targets ~150 farms in year one, and a single coastal farm alone adds 40,000/day. Total output by Ch15 should almost certainly be much higher than 130,000/day. **Fix:** either raise the Brussels cost figure substantially, or explicitly state a much larger total daily output figure and adjust "more than a week" to a smaller fraction (e.g., "half a day's output"). See current_state.md for the flag.

## Resolved decisions (for reference — don't re-litigate)

- [x] **Is Angline immortal?** No — she's mortal, just rates herself so far above everyone that she might as well be.
- [x] **How did Angline open the original portal ("the key")?** A field resonator (labeled as a ventilation unit) pushed open a dormant "thin place" using the building's substation for ~4 seconds. Explained in Ch9; the device is destroyed there.
- [x] **Why are city signs not in English?** They're in the local script; Billy runs a machine-learning translator built by Angline (no secret Earth-Green historical link).
- [x] **How does magic get explained to Bethany?** Learned by observation first (locals doing small mote-effects); the killing-based truth (harvest) revealed later by Brask, Ch7.
- [x] **Do signals pass through doors?** Yes — established Ch6 (video call) and used extensively after (fiber cable Ch7, radio sync Ch10/15).
- [x] **Silver price in Pinnacle** — confirmed ~40 motes/kg (Ch9).
- [x] **Do the Houses know great crystals are dangerous?** Yes (Ashfall/Carrow disaster, ~550 years ago) — this was a late fix to the Ch11 ending; the earlier draft implied they didn't know, which contradicted "the peace" as 500 years of deliberate restraint. Current ending has Brask tell Angline the history and she's "glad she negotiated."
- [x] **Killing people for motes** — confirmed not cost-effective (poor motes/kg vs. cost to raise a human, per Angline's own math, Ch7) and morally off the table per author. Keep as an established fact, don't revisit as a live temptation.
- [x] **Interludes (non-Angline/Bethany POV, e.g. Oren's Ch5 section)** — allowed sparingly as a device; not a hard rule violation.
