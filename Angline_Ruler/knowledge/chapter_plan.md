### Longer-range plans discussed with the author
- **Weapons lab:** radar-based detection (Shades can't hide from radio), rune mass drivers, drone swarms, reconfigurable wards. A cracked-core weapon is understood as possible but taboo — using it would break the 500-year Compact peace, which is the whole point of restraint as a story beat.
- **Space program:** spy satellite (personal-use callback), core-heated closed-cycle turbine + radiator for orbital power (no solar panels — thematically "the magnets must spin" even in space), launched from a Green-side door with no Earth tracking. Possibly a future Earth-orbit door.
- **Brask leading something more:** already achieved (rune works, Ch15) — could expand to leading the rail or another new division.
- **Cast growth:** deliberately kept small (author preference) — two mains (Angline, Bethany), strong recurring supports (Brask, Nkemelu [status uncertain], Marit, the Lady of Verdane, the Lord of Sable). Avoid adding more main-cast members casually.
