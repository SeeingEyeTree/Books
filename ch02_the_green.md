# Chapter 2: The Green

> Original rough draft (author's), unedited. The fields should become a layered ecosystem; see knowledge/continuity.md.

We emerged into a world of bamboo. If it even was bamboo it stretched 100 meters into the sky, and had a trunk the width of a plane’s fuselage. It was ridiculous. We couldn't see far and it was the only thing we could see.

I couldn’t feel unease since I was of course perfect and always in control. If I could though I would be. I knew nothing of how technologically advanced people would be here, how many there were, if they were hostile. I didn’t even know if the gravity was 9.81 m/s/s. How was one meant to make decisions if she knew nothing?

I would rectify that as quickly as possible. I had Billy send out 8 drones to explore. I had another 8 spread out in a grid around us. The most important thing right now was awareness. I would have brought a spy satellite if the government would have given me one. I could ask for almost anything I wanted but they weren’t willing to give me one for “personal use”. Just more proof that I needed to take over.

I couldn’t pay attention to all 16 data streams but Billy was handling it. So far it just looked like bamboo until the horizon. We couldn’t get a good picture of the ground from the aerial perspective because of how damn tall the bamboo was.

What was clear was that there were no signals on any frequency band that was not noise. On Earth no matter how remote you were you would have been able to measure some sort of activity on the em spectrum. Here there was nothing. That likely meant that nothing here had that capability and if they did it was probably not as far along as it was on Earth.

What did stand out was just how uniform it all was. It was all in near perfect lines and there were gaps where it looked like something would travel. This was a farm of some kind. It was truly massive. 

Finally after a few minutes of flying something new appeared on the monitors. Decapitated bamboo. It was cut down but the stalks were just left there to rot. There was another strange thing. There were translucent glowing crystals, at fixed intervals. I had one stay where near some of the crystals but had all the rest continue exploring. 

I was looking for an ocean if it existed. Water was corrosive and the ocean was volatile. It was unlikely that there would be any large form of civilization or surveillance underwater. Billy was amphibious and we could survive just fine underwater for a long time while we found out more about the world. It was the perfect place to build a power base from, if this was a world of hostile powers.

It just needed to exist and for us to not be spotted before we got there.

“Don’t you think there would be pests of some kind? If something figured out how to eat this it would have food aplenty. It doesn’t look like what everyone is doing cares about it all that much if they are just leaving it to rot on the ground. Evolution demands that something eat this.” 

“Well I can't do spectroscopy, we have visible light, and a few of the other spectrumes. What you want me to do?”

“We could use it to pursue humanity's greatest pursuit.”  
“What is that?” Bethany asks confused

“Spining magnets quickly, and consistently. In this pursuit we have dug up billions of tons of rock, made pipelines that cross continents, gone to the coldest parts of the world and remote parts of the ocean. Forced it from the air and sea(wind turbines and sea turbines). Hell we even made a mini sun just to spin a magnet, by we I mean me.”

“We truly are a strange species.”

“What about solar panels those make electricity without spinning magnets?”

“Heresy. A blaspheme. Our purpose is to spin magnets not to have sun shine on some panels. There is no art to it, no skill, a steam turbine is a beautiful engineering masterpiece, a solar panel is an affront that is good in the world.”

“Ok then. I didn’t realize you had such strong opinions about it.”

“Where is the infrastructure? All of these plants presumably need large amounts of water and nutrients, from a centralized location. Where is that?”

We should operate on the assumption that the intelligence here has nukes and is trigger happy. 

But theft. I wanted that glowing crystal. I knew exactly the person to send.

“Bethany, you should go grab one of the crystals.”

“Why?” I don’t think she would have appreciated me saying “To see if you get nuked or not” I had figured out that most people don’t appreciate the sentiment. I mean I didn’t think that whatever was here had nukes and even if they did this was clearly something of value here and nuking it would destroy all that value. Still better to assume the worst and be proven wrong. 

“Couldn’t you just send a drone?” 

“Do they have hands?” I could have sent one but they were less disposable. Operative B0 was a better choice. 

Bethany was not instantly nuked unfortunately. There would be no worthy opponent found here. No legendary battle.
